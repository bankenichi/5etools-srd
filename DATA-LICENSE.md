The required attribution is in [ATTRIBUTION.md](ATTRIBUTION.md).
Original upstream software licensing remains in [LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md).

The projection reformats the source as structured data, applies its SRD names,
removes non-SRD content and editorial additions, and records source-backed
corrections to the pinned upstream input. Its build and verification reports
describe these changes. A work-in-progress projection is not a fidelity
certificate; publication requires the documented source-verification gates.
